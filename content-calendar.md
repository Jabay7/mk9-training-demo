# MK9 Training — Instagram Content Calendar

**Cadence:** 3 posts/week, all times **Pacific (SoCal)**
**Default slots:** **Tue 6:00 PM** · **Wed 12:00 PM** · **Thu 6:30 PM**
**Format priority:** Reels first (growth), carousels second (saves), single image last.

> After ~3 weeks, check **Instagram Insights → Most Active Times** and shift these slots to match your real audience. Benchmarks get you started; your data wins.

---

## The content mix (the "4 buckets")

Rotate these so you're never just selling. Aim roughly: 40% proof, 30% education, 20% story/brand, 10% offer.

| Bucket | Why it works | Example |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| 🦮 **Proof** (before/after, transformations) | Sells without selling; builds trust | "Reactive on day 1 → loose leash on day 14" |
| 🎓 **Education** (quick tips) | Saves + shares = reach | "3 mistakes keeping your dog from listening" |
| 🇺🇸 **Story/Brand** (veteran, the bond, who you are) | Builds the emotional connection that converts | "Why I train owners, not just dogs" |
| 📣 **Offer** (book the free assessment) | Turns followers into clients | "2 assessment spots open this week" |

**Every caption ends with one clear CTA:** *"DM 'BOND' or tap the link to book your free assessment."*

## WEEK 1 — Establish who you are

**Tue 6:00 PM · Reel · Story/Brand**
- **Hook (first 1.5s):** "Veteran owned. Built on one thing most trainers skip."
- **Content:** 15–25s of you working a dog, text overlay tells your story → "the bond between you and your dog is everything."
- **Caption:** Short origin story. CTA to book.
- **Audio:** trending audio, low volume under your voiceover.

**Wed 12:00 PM · Carousel · Education**
- **Slide 1 (hook):** "3 reasons your dog ignores you"
- **Slides 2–4:** one reason each (no structure / no trust / inconsistent owner)
- **Slide 5:** "Fixable. Here's where we start →" + CTA
- *Carousels get saved — saves are gold for reach.*

**Thu 6:30 PM · Reel · Proof**
- **Hook:** "Day 1 vs Day 14 — same dog."
- Split-screen / before-after clip. Let the transformation speak.
- **Caption:** What changed wasn't the dog — it was the relationship. CTA.

## WEEK 2 — Show the method

**Tue 6:00 PM · Reel · Education**
- **Hook:** "Stop repeating commands. Do this instead."
- One quick, genuinely useful tip (e.g., say it once, follow through).
- CTA: "Want this dialed in for your dog? Free assessment in bio."

**Wed 12:00 PM · Reel · Story/Brand**
- **Hook:** "I don't just train dogs. I train owners."
- Explain the Day Program philosophy — dog learns all day, goes home, YOU keep building the bond.
- This positions your #1 differentiator.

**Thu 6:30 PM · Carousel · Proof + Offer**
- Client win / testimonial as a carousel (quote slide → before/after → "book yours").
- If no testimonials yet: use a "here's what 2 weeks looks like" progress carousel.

## WEEK 3 — Serve your community

**Tue 6:00 PM · Reel · Story/Brand (First Responders)**
- **Hook:** "To everyone who runs toward the danger — this one's for you."
- Speak directly to military, vets, police, fire, EMT, nurses, doctors.
- Announce priority scheduling + dedicated pricing. This post will get shared in those communities.

**Wed 12:00 PM · Reel · Education**
- **Hook:** "The #1 thing owners get wrong on walks."
- Quick fix tip. End with the bond message.

**Thu 6:30 PM · Reel · Proof**
- A "problem dog" win — reactivity, pulling, no recall → resolved.
- **Hook:** "They said this dog was 'too far gone.'"

## Quick rules that matter more than perfect timing

1. **Consistency > frequency.** 3 solid posts every week beats 7 then nothing.
2. **First 1.5 seconds decide everything** on a Reel. Lead with the hook, not a logo.
3. **Reply to every comment & DM fast** — the algorithm rewards it and DMs are where bookings happen.
4. **Reuse winners.** A Reel that pops? Make 3 more like it.
5. **Always be capturing.** Film every session (with client OK). Raw before/after clips are your best content.
