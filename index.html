Questions

Straight answers

Why do you only offer two programs?

Because focus gets results. We pour everything into doing two things exceptionally well rather than offering a menu of mediocre options. Most dogs thrive in the Day Program; Board & Train is there for the cases that truly need it.

How do you decide between Day Program and Board & Train?

It's case by case, decided at your free transformation session. We look at temperament, behavior, your goals, and your home environment, then give you the honest best fit, even if it's the simpler, less expensive option.

What do you mean by "the bond"?

The relationship between you and your dog. We train owners as seriously as we train dogs, because lasting obedience comes from trust and leadership, not just commands. A strong bond is the single most important thing we ask you to bring.

Do you really offer priority and discounts for first responders?

Yes, and we mean all of them: military, veterans, police, firefighters, EMTs, paramedics, doctors, and nurses. Mention it when you apply for front of line scheduling and dedicated pricing.

Is my dog too aggressive / too old / too far gone?

Let us be the judge, kindly and honestly. Many "problem" dogs simply never had structure or a real bond. Book the free transformation session and we'll tell you the truth about what's possible.
